02 / The workflow

From dimensions
to a client proposal.

Keep your team focused on the client. We prepare the design options and supporting information for your next quotation.

01 — BRIEF

Tell us what you need.

Send the footprint, open sides, client logo and the modular system you use. Include any storage, display or furniture requirements.

02 — DESIGN

Compare the alternatives.

Review twelve concepts for the same space and brand. Compare silhouettes, display areas and layouts in one presentation sheet.

03 — PROPOSAL

Present it as your own.

Receive the presentation and parts breakdown with your company identity. Quotation figures use your rates when supplied.
