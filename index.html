The system
40 × 40 mm aluminium profiles, 1.00 m bay pitch, SEG fabric frames, post heights 2.5 to 4.0 m. It maps onto Octanorm, beMatrix, SYMA and similar stock.
The check
Every design is a geometric fit and collision check: posts, beams, panels, counter, screen, furniture. Structural engineering stays with the builder.
The prices
Your rate card is loaded once. From then on every proposal carries your prices, your margin and your company footer.
The output
PDF, PNG sheets, CSV and JSON. Nothing to install, nothing to learn. You send a size, you receive a proposal.
